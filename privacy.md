This application is an experimental tool intended solely for its author's private use. It is not a public service. If you are not the author, do not use the application, authorize it to access your accounts, or submit information to it.

The application may collect, access, store, and process any information supplied to it or made available through authorized accounts, connected services, and its operating environment. This may include account information, calendars, events, documents, messages, prompts, and technical logs, depending on its configuration and permissions.

The author may use that information for personal tasks, automation, experimentation, debugging, and other purposes the author considers appropriate, subject to applicable law and service-provider requirements. Information may be transmitted to AI providers and other connected services as part of those activities.

## No confidentiality commitment

Do not treat this application as a confidential or privacy-preserving service. It offers no application-specific guarantees of confidentiality, limited retention, deletion on request, or continued availability. Information may be retained indefinitely, appear in logs or backups, or be disclosed through configured sharing and publishing features. Connected providers apply their own terms and privacy policies.

Do not submit sensitive information or information you are not authorized to share. This notice does not authorize access beyond permissions granted by an account owner, waive anyone's legal rights, or override applicable laws or Google's and other providers' data-use requirements.

## Revoking access

Google account access can be revoked through [Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections). Revocation does not automatically delete information already stored or shared.

The application and this policy may change as the author's experiments evolve. For questions, use the contact information on the [application homepage](./).
